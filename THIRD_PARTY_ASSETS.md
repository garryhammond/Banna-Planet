# Third-party assets

## Rigged monkey

- Asset: **Monkey 3D model. Rigged. FBX**
- Author: **tomk**
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/monkey-3d-model-rigged-fbx
- Download used: `Monkey_animated.zip`
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain dedication**
- Attribution: not required. This notice is retained for provenance.
- Local files: `assets/models/cc0-monkey/`

The source archive contains a textured skinned FBX monkey with 34 bones and one
animation take. Banana Planet uses that take for ordinary locomotion and keeps
its original articulated procedural ape as the fallback and for gameplay poses
that the downloaded asset does not provide (trip, fall, hit and recovery).
